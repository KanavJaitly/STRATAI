# P11-FB2: the alliance-aware fallback rule (decided 2026-11-14; frozen before any replay or comparison)

**Decided by:** Kanav (owner), 2026-11-14. He approved the follow-up plan and this definition, with one change: the
consistency factor uses 0.25, not the 0.5 first proposed, because consistency is computed from alliance scores and is
schedule-confounded.

**Countersignature (Sven Morais):** ______________________ (date: __________)

**Status:**
- **Decided by Kanav; not accepted and not validated.**
- FB2 is served `not_validated` with no probability, and its projected score is `not_validated`.
- Nothing here changes a gate, the P6-M7 engine, the draft model, or any model.

**What it replaces:**
- FB2 replaces P11-FB1 as the fallback served when the engine refuses (P11-Q1).
- FB1's record, `.agent/phase11/decisions/P11_Q1_Q4_DECISIONS_2026-11-04.md`, is write-once and is not edited.
- FB1 stays in the code as a pure function (`fallback_lists`), used by the tests and by the FB1/FB2 comparison only.

**Frozen.**
- This file is committed before any FB2 replay, comparison or measurement on real data.
- After the comparison, FB2 is not tuned.
- Any change to an input, formula, weight, threshold or tie-break is a new rule, P11-FB2.1, with its own dated record.

**Design input, disclosed:**
- Kanav's human replay of 2024njfla as team 11 on 2026-11-14 informed this design.
- The 5624 investigation found no data defect. FB1 was ordering the tail of the list by schedule-dependent alliance
  averages, with no notion of the alliance being built.
- 2024njfla is therefore not independent evidence about FB2.

## 1. Where FB2 sits (P11-Q1, unchanged)

1. The P6-M7 engine first, unchanged. When it serves, its ranking is served and FB2 does not run.
2. Only when the engine refuses: FB2, with the engine's refusal shown beside it.
3. The sources are never mixed. A response's list comes entirely from one source (`source` = `engine_p6_m7` or
   `fallback_p11_fb2`).

FB2 orders only at the event's selection moment, like FB1: one minute after the last scheduled qualification match
(`event_selection_moment`). Before that moment it is withheld (`selection_time_unavailable`).

## 2. Inputs, all strictly before the selection moment `T`

1. **The record's real state** (`workspaces.draft_log.DraftView`): its alliances, declines, picks made, rules, final
   qualification ranks and pool.
2. **The alliance FB2 advises**, chosen as FB1 chooses it (`selecting_seed`): the one the team is on, or the one it is
   projected to captain.
   - **Members** are that alliance's recorded members (captain first, then picks).
   - When the team is a projected captain with no recorded alliance yet, the members are the team itself.
3. **Per-robot component estimates s_c(t)**, from the P11A A3 estimator, used alone.
   - `ml/alliance/estimator.py` is copied unchanged from `p11a/recommendation-intelligence` (version
     `p11a-a3-v1`), with `fit_component(component, rows, y, teams)`:
     - no individual prior (`individual=None`), so every robot gets the current-event population prior, with μ
       integrated;
     - the default `EstimatorPolicy`, whose label thresholds are `candidate_unapproved`; the labels are shown on
       cards and never change the ranking.
   - **Not used:** P11A's season scoring configurations, `data/season_scoring/`, E5, prior-event individual priors,
     drift variance and plan search. P11A stays disabled.
   - **Rows:**
     - every qualification match at the event with `scheduled_time < T` and both alliance scores recorded;
     - one row per alliance, naming its three teams;
     - **excluded:** an alliance whose current TBA payload lists any `dq_team_keys` for it;
     - surrogate appearances are kept.
   - **Components:**
     - **component mode** (`auto`, `teleop`, `endgame`): the P5-M7 adapter
       (`ml.features.score_components.score_components`), seasons 2024–2026, fouls excluded. Rows without a TBA
       breakdown for that alliance are left out of the component fits.
     - **total mode** (one component, `total`): the alliance's official score, fouls included, because no breakdown
       separates them.
   - **Which mode:**
     - component mode, when the season is supported, at least 12 rows have a breakdown, and none of the three fits
       raises `EstimatorError` or is degenerate (σ̂² on the optimizer bound);
     - otherwise total mode, on all rows, which needs at least 12 rows and a non-degenerate fit;
     - otherwise FB2 is withheld (`fb2_projection_unavailable`), with the reason.
   - **The P5-M4 switch rule** (`serving_switch`): the at-event estimates are used once every rostered team has played
     its ⌈n/2⌉-th qualification match before `T`. At the selection moment this has always happened, except in a
     schedule anomaly.
     - If it has not happened, FB2 uses each team's EPA components (`epa_auto`, `epa_teleop`, `epa_endgame`, from the
       served D18 EPA source in the same features).
     - Teams without all three EPA components are unranked.
4. **Phase 3 reliability and consistency** (`reliability_score`, `consistency_rating`) at `T`.
   - They come from the same point-in-time features as FB1's P5-M4 analysis (`analyze_event` at `T`), never from the
     current-state `team_metrics` table.
   - Each is absent below Phase 3's minimum, and an absent value is never imputed.
5. **Defense** (P6-Q11, quality only) at `T`, when its scouting summary is sufficient (Phase 3's two-observation
   minimum). It is informational only (§6).
6. **Projected captains** (`DraftView.projected_captains`), for the decline flag (§7).

## 3. Formula

Notation: C is the set of components (component mode {auto, teleop, endgame}; total mode {total}). M is the members
with an estimate.

- **Estimated contribution:** `s(t) = Σ_c s_c(t)`.
- **Benchmark per component:**
  - K = min(24, the number of teams with an estimate);
  - `B_c` = the median of `s_c` over the K teams with the highest `s(t)`, roughly a playoff-level robot.
  - It depends only on the event at `T`, not on the record's state.
- **Gap per component:** `gap_c = max(0, B_c − mean_{m∈M} s_c(m))`. It is 0 when M is empty.
- **Reliability and consistency factor for a candidate t:**
  `ρ(t) = (reliability(t) / 100) × (1 − 0.25 × (1 − consistency(t) / 100))`.
  - An absent reliability contributes 1, and so does an absent consistency.
  - The card says which is absent. Nothing is imputed.
- **Effective contribution:** `eff_c(t) = s_c(t) × ρ(t)`.
- **Fit:** `fit_c(t) = min(max(eff_c(t), 0), gap_c)`, and `Fit(t) = Σ_c fit_c(t)`.
  - The candidate fills at most the gap, and a negative estimate fills nothing.
- **Rank score:** `R(t) = Σ_{m∈M} s(m) + Σ_c eff_c(t) + 0.5 × Fit(t)`.
  - The members' total is the same for every candidate, so it does not change the order. It is in R so that R reads
    as a projected alliance quantity.
- **Projected alliance score (shown on the card, never adjusted by ρ or Fit):**
  - `V(t)` = the sum of `s(u)` over the members with an estimate, plus t;
  - when that is more than three robots (an alliance of four), only the best three by `s(u)` count;
  - when the alliance is still incomplete, V covers only the members plus t, and the card names exactly those
    robots; it never assumes a later pick;
  - fouls are excluded in component mode and included in total mode, and the card says which;
  - V is labelled `not_validated` and gives no interval.
- **Weights:** fit weight 0.5; consistency weight 0.25; K = 24. These are judgment calls, not fitted to any data.

## 4. Ranking, lists and exclusions

- **Candidates:** the available teams (`DraftView.availability`) minus FB1's exclusions (`_excluded`, unchanged):
  - your own team;
  - your alliance's members;
  - projected captains of alliances whose first turn comes before your next turn.
- **Unranked:** a candidate with no estimate (no row at the event, or, before the switch, no EPA components) is listed
  as unranked and never recommended.
- **Order:** R descending, then V descending, then qualification rank ascending (no rank last), then team number
  ascending.
- **Primary** is the first candidate. **Backups** are the next `max(3, m)`, where m is the other alliances' picks before
  this alliance's next turn, as in FB1.
- **Movement:** the same fixed templates as FB1 (`movement`), comparing FB2's list before and after the latest entry.
- **Candidate projections:** every available team (ranked candidates, excluded teams with their exclusion reason,
  unranked teams with "no estimate"), so the profile drawer can show any team's projection and reasons.

## 5. Card reasons (fixed templates; no free text, no LLM)

Every primary and backup card, and every candidate projection, carries these lines, in this order, where they apply.
Numbers are shown to one decimal place.

1. `Projected alliance score {V} with teams {robots} ({fouls note}; not validated).`
2. `Estimated contribution {s} pts: {component} {s_c}, ….`
3. One of:
   - `Fills {fit_c} of a {gap_c}-pt {component} gap.` (one line for each component with gap_c > 0 and fit_c > 0);
   - `Adds nothing to your {component} gap.` (for each component with gap_c > 0 and fit_c = 0);
   - `Your alliance has no gap against this event's playoff-level benchmark.` (when every gap_c = 0).
4. Reliability and consistency, one of:
   - `Reliability {r}, consistency {k} (n = {n}): counted at {ρ}×.`;
   - `Reliability absent ({reason}): not adjusted for it.` and/or `Consistency absent ({reason}): not adjusted for
     it.`
5. Defense, one of:
   - `Defense: no scouting.`;
   - `Defense {d}/5 from {n} observations (descriptive; it does not change the ranking).`
6. `May decline (projected captain of alliance {N}).` (only when §7 applies).
7. `Qualification rank {r} of {N}.`, or `No qualification rank in the record.`
   - When r > ⌈N/2⌉, a second line: `Ranked in the lower half, but its estimated contribution is {k} of {M} at this
     event; qualification rank also reflects partners, schedule and ranking points.`
8. `Estimate labels: {label: reason}; …` (when the estimator labels it: `prior_dominated`, `weakly_identified`,
   `collapse_screen_flagged`).
9. One mode line:
   - `Ranked on the total score only: {reason}.` (in total mode);
   - `Ranked on EPA components: the P5-M4 switch point has not passed.` (before the switch).

The record's movement lines are kept unchanged.

## 6. Defense

- Shown on every card: either its value, when the scouting summary is sufficient, or `Defense: no scouting`.
- **No ranking effect.**
  - P6-Q11 keeps defense descriptive while its definition is pending.
  - There is no measured link from defense to points (the P6-M10 zero-effect rule).

## 7. Decline flag

- A candidate that is a projected captain of an alliance N other than yours, and is not excluded under §4, carries
  `May decline (projected captain of alliance N)`.
- **It informs only. It does not change the ranking, and no flagged team is excluded.** Any penalty would be an
  unvalidated pick or decline probability, and there is no pick model (P6-Q6).

## 8. Output, status and speed

- **Source:** `fallback_p11_fb2`. **Status:** `not_validated`, with no probability.
- **Validation reasons** state the rule, the estimator and its open questions (§9), and the engine's refusal.
- **Provenance:** the rule, this record's path, `estimator_version`, the mode, the as-of, the switch state, the
  benchmark per component, and the record sequence.
- **Contract:** the fields permitted by `.agent/phase11/decisions/P11_FU_CONTRACT_2026-11-14.md`.
- **Speed (P11-Q3):** ≤ 2 s at p95.
  - The event estimates (three REML fits, or one) and the P5-M4 analysis are computed once per (event, moment, models,
    EPA source) and cached like FB1's ordering. A recorded action only re-ranks.
  - Cold and warm timings are both reported.

## 9. Open methodology questions that apply to FB2 (from P11A; unresolved)

- **The prior.** Every robot gets the current-event population prior (normal, with τ² fitted by REML). Whether that
  prior is right for FRC robots, and how much it shrinks strong or weak teams, is an open P11A question (A3.2).
- **Component independence.** Auto, teleop and endgame are fitted separately, as if independent. They are not
  independent in play: a robot's autonomous routine shapes its teleop.
- **No per-robot ground truth.** Only alliance totals are observed (E1b), so no individual estimate can be validated.
  The E2 benchmark's no-foul MAE (30.2 for the joint decomposition, against 44.3 for team averages) is evidence about
  alliance-score prediction, not about individual robots or about pick quality.
- **The collapse screen is non-authoritative.** Short schedules can collapse σ̂² undetected. The labels are shown on
  cards, and nothing is gated on them.
- **The weights** (0.5, 0.25, K = 24) are judgment, not estimates.

For these reasons FB2 is `not_validated`. The comparison in §10 is descriptive and is not a validation.

## 10. The comparison after the freeze (descriptive; no tuning)

- **Script:** `scripts/phase11_replay.py`, with the samples Phase 11 used (`--per-season 2`, seed 20261104, and the
  30-event wide sample).
- **Database:** only an isolated copy (`stratai_test_p11fu`); its difference from `stratai_test_p11` is reported.
- **Measures:** FB1 and FB2 run on the same copy. For each real pick, the measure is where the picked team appeared in
  the list advising the picking alliance just before the pick: primary, backup position, or unlisted. It is reported
  overall and per season.
- **2024njfla specifics:** team 11's two picks, and the position of 2577 under each rule.
- **P11-DM1** (the replayed record matches TBA) must still hold under FB2. The record replay does not depend on the
  fallback rule, so this is a regression check.
- **Interpretation:** real teams pick for reasons no rule sees, so how often a rule lists the actual pick is a
  description of agreement with human picks, not a measure of pick quality.
