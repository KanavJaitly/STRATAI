## 1. HD-1: a declining team can still become captain, but can never be picked

The two parts are separate schema fields:
- `declined_team_may_be_picked_later` = **false**;
- `declined_team_may_become_captain` = **true** (v2).

The consumers keep them apart:
- `available_for_pick` blocks decliners from picks;
- `_captain` admits decliners as captains;
- `test_hd1_and_hd2_hold_in_the_draft_model` checks both on a synthetic draft.
