**Why C1 v1 is invalid as written:**
- **It contradicts its own stated basis.** It says the slice is used "as in M7".
- **M7's frozen procedure is out-of-sample.**
  - `ml/calibration/calibrator.py::fit_calibrated_win_prob_model` sorts its training rows by time and fits the model on the first 80% only.
  - It fits the calibrator on that model's predictions of the held-out last 20%, "keeping the calibrator's own fitting data strictly separate from whatever model.fit() saw".
  - The Phase 4 M7 spec says the same: `.agent/phase4/M05_M07_REDESIGN_SPEC.md` §2.1, "the model is fit on the first 80% (temporal); the calibrator on the last 20%".
- **In-sample predictions bias the calibrator.** They are systematically better fitted than real out-of-sample outputs, so the calibrator would learn a mapping that does not hold for 2027 predictions.

**Cause.** I wrote C1 from P6-M0's note that "as in M7, the calibration slice lies inside the model's training period". I read "training period" as compatible with the slice being in the training **rows**. M7 keeps the slice inside the training **period** but **out of the model's fit**.

**Timing.** The defect was detectable at freeze: `px1b-v1` was registered at 2026-10-09T01:08:51Z (`d45432e`, 21:09 −04:00), before C1 was frozen at `021e6ea` (2026-10-08T21:42:10−04:00).

**The same reading affects the frozen PX-2.** That is P6-Q4 / `P6_M2_PX1_SPEC.md` §5: PX-1 fit on all 2024–2025 rows, with the slice = the last 20% of 2025.
- **Observation only.** That spec is frozen and blocked by D9 (PX-1 failed), and is not changed here.
- Any PX-1-chain run would need the same correction as a versioned amendment first.

**No real PX-2b run is possible or was attempted.** `px2b` refuses at the missing 2027 ruleset, and then at PX-1b's acceptance. Nothing was calibrated, and no outcome was inspected.

**No code change was made,** on Kanav's instruction to stop and report. The C4 `px2b` code path (`b965210`) implements v1's in-sample procedure. It must be updated to the adopted correction before any PX-2b run; it is unreachable until then.

## 2. Proposed correction (versioned; NOT adopted)

**Document:** `.agent/phase6/P6_M3_PX2B_SPEC_V2.md`.
- **Status:** proposed, committed so that its blob is fixed. It governs PX-2b only if Kanav approves that exact blob. v1 stays in the repository unchanged, superseded only on approval.
- **The change:** the calibration predictions come from a **calibration-base model**, fit by PX-1b's frozen spec §2–§4 on the eligible rows that:
  - (a) are strictly earlier than the slice's first row;
  - (b) belong to no event with a slice row. Event-disjoint, because 11 events straddle the cut.

  This is M7's existing out-of-sample procedure, applied to PX-1b.
- **Unchanged:** the slice (the same 526 rows), the calibrator class, the gate (G1–G4, every constant), the 2027 holdout, the prerequisites, the records.
- **Must be frozen before any 2027 evaluation outcome exists.** Today none exists.

## 3. Correction of the specification-freeze statement

**The inaccuracy.** My previous report said the three specs (`021e6ea`) "were frozen before any of their inputs or outcomes exist". For C1 that was inaccurate. Each spec's own header says "before any … input or outcome exists". The PX-2b header is inaccurate in the same way, and the PX-4b and M8b headers are imprecise.

**The precise statement:** they were frozen **before any 2027 evaluation input or outcome existed**, but **after** their 2024–2026 development inputs existed and after 2026 outcomes had been observed.

The commits, timestamps and blobs are unchanged; nothing is rewritten.

| Spec (freeze commit, time) | **Existed at freeze** | **Did not exist at freeze** |
|---|---|---|
| PX-1b v1 (`ca13e98`, 2026-10-08T21:02:49−04:00) | 2024–2026 playoff rows and outcomes. 2026 outcomes had already been **observed** (PX-1 run 1 and rerun1). The PX-1 audit and the label-free scale audit | any PX-1b fit or prediction; any 2027 data |
| PX-2b v1, PX-4b v1, M8b v1 (`021e6ea`, 2026-10-08T21:42:10−04:00) | All of the above, plus `px1b-v1` and its seed-only comparator, registered at 2026-10-09T01:08:51Z (`d45432e`), trained on 2024–2026 **including the PX-2b slice rows**. DEV-1 results (`cb41311`). M6/M7 `d18`, `ranking_xgb_v2` `d18`, the P5-M4 policy | any 2027 data, ruleset or outcome; PX-1b acceptance (FINAL-1); the PX-2b calibrator; the 2027 P6-M1 record; the M8b pre-run record; a confirmed mentor |

**So for C1:** its 2026 calibration inputs (rows, features, labels) existed, had been observed, and were in the model's training fit. Only its 2027 evaluation inputs and outcomes did not exist.
