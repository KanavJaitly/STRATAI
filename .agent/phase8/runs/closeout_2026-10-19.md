## R11: A4 print. Fixed and verified for all locally servable content

**Method.** Headless Chromium through the DevTools Protocol, `Page.printToPDF` at exact A4 (8.2677 × 11.6929 in,
0.4 in margins). Each PDF was rendered page by page (PyMuPDF, in a throwaway environment outside the project) and
**every page was inspected**. Text blocks were checked against the right page edge, and elements were measured
against the printable width under print media.

**Before** (`report_red_A4_before.pdf`, `958bcf5e7265e024`): 16 pages. All content was present and nothing was
clipped. But `.card { break-inside: avoid }` pushed whole cards to new pages, which left two near-empty pages (3 and
4). Each robot's evidence card spilled its feeding row onto an extra page (6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16). Team headings were
orphaned at the bottom of pages, and a table row was split across a page break.

**Fix** (`frontend/src/styles/dashboard.css`, `@media print`):
- `.card` may break across pages;
- `tr`, the not-produced items, notices, `.facts` entries, card heads and the report footer are kept whole;
- `h1`–`h4`, `details > summary` and card heads have `break-after: avoid`.

**After:**
- `report_red_A4_final.pdf` (`297d59210ce56d59`) and `report_blue_A4_final.pdf` (`4abcebb2c872b371`): 10 pages each,
  page size 595.9 × 841.9 pt (A4).
- No text block reaches the right edge, and 0 elements are wider than the 703 px printable width.
- All 5 report sections and all 6 not-produced items are present, and labels and the visible raw reason codes are
  readable.
- No split rows, and no orphaned headings or footer.
- `beforeprint` fired once and opened all 6 collapsed sections; `afterprint` closed them again.
- The blue-side report shows the blue alliance as "our alliance".

**Regression tests:** two tests in `accessibility.r9.test.tsx` ("report print pagination"), proven to fail against
the previous stylesheet.

**Residual (not verified):** model-populated content (strategy tables, roles, a win-probability value) cannot be
produced locally (`model_not_loaded`), so it has not been printed. It depends on P8-R1.
